The low-water rate spike came two months early

Barge freight on the lower Mississippi jumped 24 percent in the last full week of July, a move this river usually saves for late September.

Grain-barge freight on the lower Mississippi River reached $18.09 a ton in the week reported July 28: up $3.55 in one week and 49.5 percent over four weeks, averaged across ten lower-river segments. Moves of that size are not rare on this river. Moves of that size in July are.

Set aside 2004, the series' first year, when it was swinging between bases of $6.43 and $10.55, and this is the biggest early-summer weekly move the U.S. Department of Agriculture has recorded since the series began.
