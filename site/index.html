The rate eased for a second week; Marseilles is still the binding lock

The system average is down about 2% to $13.46 a ton, corn export demand holds firm, and the forward read is withheld as too stale to publish.

The system-average modeled rate slipped to $13.46 a ton in the rate-week ending June 23, 2026, from $13.76 the week before — about 2% lower — and the average pressure index fell from 40.2 to 36.1. That is an elevated market cooling at the margin, not a break.

The Illinois system stays the tightest corridor and Marseilles stays the binding lock, at 13.2 hours average wait on June 26. The forward read is withheld this week: the forward leg's newest snapshot was 25 days old at publication, past the freshness line. Read the note.
